Questions about readings, purchases, crystals or your account? Write to terlan_924@mail.ru. We usually answer within 2 working days. You can also use Profile → Contact support in the app.

Common questions

How do I cancel Premium? On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Mystic Eye → Cancel Subscription.

I bought something but don't see it. Open Profile → Restore purchases. If crystals are still missing, write to us with the date of the purchase.

How do I delete my account? Profile → Delete my account. Your data is permanently removed from our server.

Are readings real predictions? No. Mystic Eye is for entertainment only. AI fortune tellers are always marked AI.
