Privacy Policy

Kismet: Coffee & Tarot · Effective 23 November 2026

Kismet is a fortune-telling app for entertainment. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how you can delete it.

Who we are

Kismet is operated by Terlan Hacizade, Azerbaijan. Contact: terlan_924@mail.ru.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address or phone number, or the identifier from Sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Details you enter: first name (and last name if you add it), birth date, zodiac sign, birth time and place (astrology only), gender, love status, partner name and birthday (love match only).
  • Reading content: photos of your coffee cup, saucer or palm, your questions and wishes, dreams, and the readings you save.
  • Live reader: chat messages, photos and voice messages you send to the real Fortune Teller, session times and ratings.
  • Purchases: which products you bought and your crystal history. Payments are handled by Apple; we never see your card.
  • Voice: when you speak a question, it is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition (on your device or by Apple). We keep only the text, not the recording (voice messages you choose to send to the Fortune Teller are kept as sent).

We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell your data.

Why we use it

  • To create your account and keep your readings, crystals and Premium status.
  • To write your AI readings and, if you allow it, to translate your live chat.
  • To run live sessions with the Fortune Teller and bill crystals fairly.
  • To answer support requests and handle reports of abuse.

AI readings (Anthropic)

Before any photo, question or birth data is sent to AI, the app asks for your permission. If you allow it, the data needed for that reading is sent securely to our AI provider, Anthropic (Claude), only to write your reading. Anthropic does not use it to train its models. You can turn AI readings off at any time in Profile → AI readings. With AI off, built-in readings are shown and nothing is sent to Anthropic.

Live chat translation: if AI readings are on, your chat messages are translated by Anthropic so the Fortune Teller can read them in her language. If AI is off, your messages are delivered without translation.

Who can see your data

  • Supabase (our server and database provider) stores your account and data.
  • Anthropic processes AI readings and translations, only with your permission.
  • Apple processes payments and Sign in with Apple. Google processes Google sign-in if you use it.
  • The Fortune Teller (a real person) sees your first name and what you send her in a live session or written reading.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, readings, chats, photos, voice messages and crystal history are permanently deleted from our server. Copies in our provider's backups expire on their normal schedule.

Your rights

  • Download: Profile → Download my data.
  • Delete: Profile → Delete my account. An active subscription must be cancelled in your Apple ID settings.
  • Correct: Profile → Edit details.
  • You can also write to us at terlan_924@mail.ru.

Children

Kismet is rated 17+ and is not meant for children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 17.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell you in the app when the change is important.
